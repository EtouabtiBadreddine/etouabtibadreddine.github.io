Featured · Alexsys Solutions · 2026

PharmaMind

A smart drug catalogue with an AI assistant that answers questions only from official medication leaflets, cites its sources, and runs entirely on a local model so patient data never leaves the machine.

  • Anti-hallucination guardrails Answers come only from the leaflets, otherwise "information not found", with the source excerpt shown.
  • PDF ingestion pipeline pypdf extraction, split by leaflet section (indications, dosage, contraindications), then vector-indexed.
  • Interaction checker Flask app in 3 layers with SQLAlchemy + SQLite, multi-role login and alerts ranked by severity.
  • RAG
  • Ollama
  • Qwen2.5-7B
  • bge-m3
  • NumPy
  • Flask
  • pypdf

How it answers a question

  1. 01QuestionAsked in plain words
  2. 02EmbeddingTurned into a vector with bge-m3
  3. 03SearchCosine similarity over leaflet passages
  4. 04Top 4The closest passages are kept
  5. 05Qwen2.5-7BRuns locally through Ollama
  6. 06AnswerWritten from the passages, with sources

› Can I take ibuprofen with an anticoagulant?

Caution. The leaflet lists anticoagulants under interactions: combined with NSAIDs like ibuprofen, the risk of bleeding rises. Ask a doctor or pharmacist first.
